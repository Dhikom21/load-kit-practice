The Load Scenario Kit — project brief

Course D0020E · 5 groups · you build the intelligence layer on a working Kubernetes load-testing kit

What the project is

One machine. Three kinds of real work fighting over it: batch jobs (a backup that must finish by 2 am), a data stream (events arriving, buffered, processed), and user traffic (people waiting for a response).

The kit puts all three on a single Kubernetes node and scripts a repeatable storm — a burst at minute 40, a big job at minute 60 — on a real cluster. It measures everything every 15 s and writes down every real event, with its cause, in a diary.

The kit you are handed is complete and working: engine, scenarios, UI, CI pipeline. What is missing is the intelligence layer — the four things an on-call engineer actually wants, and what your group will build:

  • Scaler — how many pods do I need, right now?
  • Forecaster — what will the metric look like next?
  • Early-warning detector — is something about to go wrong?
  • Cause-finder — when it went wrong, who caused it?

What you build — and how it is measured

The forecaster and the forecast-bands UI panel (shipped and enabled) are one feature seen from two ends — better model, better panel. Full contracts: students/TASK.md.
